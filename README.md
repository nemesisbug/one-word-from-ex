An entry for the [One Word From Your Ex](https://make.fun/bounty/u-brave-eagle-12-one-word-from-your-ex-cfd97ba2) bounty on make.fun: send exactly one word — the word your ex would use to describe you.

## The word

**almost**

Almost the one. Almost good enough. Almost ready, almost honest, almost over her — almost, and then the door closed anyway. An ex does not call you a villain; she calls you the thing you nearly were, and that is worse, because it is true and you know it. It lands instantly, needs no explanation, and cannot be swapped for a near synonym without losing its edge: "nearly" is a weather report, "almost" is a verdict. Everyone has been somebody's almost — that is why the word gets repeated.

The bounty judges words two at a time in blind rounds, and on an exact tie the smaller text places first — six letters is about as small as a breakup can get. The word also survives the judge's first test on its own: it is exactly one word a real ex would actually say, not a phrase, not an insult, not a slogan.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) writes the word to `/output/entry.txt` — exactly one word, six characters, no trailing newline — after a self-check that it is a single word of 1–24 characters, as the gate requires.
